## Practitioner Summary
This statute mandates the adoption of written, legally vetted personnel policies for county employees. It serves as the primary defense mechanism against wrongful termination, wage-and-hour disputes, and Title VII discrimination claims by establishing objective procedural baselines.

## Key Practice Points
* **Mandatory Adoption**: County officials must adopt base personnel policies and file them with the county clerk.
* **Legal Review**: Policies must be reviewed and approved by a licensed attorney for compliance with state and federal employment laws before implementation.
* **Default Policies**: If an individual county constitutional officer fails to adopt their own policies, their employees automatically fall under the general base personnel policies adopted by the county legislative body.

## Statutory Text
(a) Each county official shall adopt base personnel policies, which shall be approved by an attorney as provided in this chapter and filed in the office of the county clerk as provided in this section on or before December 31, 1997. The county official shall submit the base personnel policies to the attorney selected in accordance with § 5-23-105, to be reviewed for compliance with this chapter and other applicable law... The county official shall submit the approved policies to the county legislative body for inclusion in the minutes of the body and filing in the office of the county clerk.
(d)(1) The county mayor and the county legislative body shall provide for the adoption of base personnel policies to govern all county employees except those governed by separate base personnel policies adopted as provided in this section.
