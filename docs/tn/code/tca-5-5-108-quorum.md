## Practitioner Summary
The simplest but most vital rule in the book: you cannot conduct business without a majority. This section defines a quorum as a simple majority of the total membership.

## Key Practice Points
* **Majority Threshold**: A quorum is defined as a majority of the *members* (not just those present at a given moment, but the body as a whole).
* **Business Necessity**: No business can be legally transacted in regular or special sessions without this quorum being met.

## Statutory Text
A majority of the members of the county legislative body of each county shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of all business by the bodies in regular or special sessions.
