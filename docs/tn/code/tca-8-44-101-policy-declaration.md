## Practitioner Summary
This section establishes the core public policy and legislative intent behind the Tennessee Open Meetings Act (TOMA). It explicitly declares that the formation of public policy and governmental decisions are public business and must not be conducted in secret.

## Key Practice Points
* **Anti-Secrecy Mandate**: The fundamental rule of TOMA is that government business cannot be conducted behind closed doors.
* **Statutory Intent**: Courts rely on this declaration to broadly construe the Open Meetings Act in favor of public access.

## Statutory Text
(a) The general assembly hereby declares it to be the policy of this state that the formation of public policy and decisions is public business and shall not be conducted in secret. 
(b) This part shall not be construed to limit any of the rights and privileges contained in the Constitution of Tennessee, Article I, § 19.
