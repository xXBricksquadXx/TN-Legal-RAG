# Tenn. Code Ann. § 10-7-513 — Military discharge records (DD-214); confidentiality; access; redaction

## Plain-language rule

- A recorded **DD-214** (military discharge record) is **confidential for seventy-five (75) years** from the date of recording, subject to statutory access rules.
- The statute describes who may inspect or obtain copies during the confidentiality period and allows removal/redaction of certain personally identifying information (e.g., SSN), subject to the statute’s process.

## Practice notes

- This is a common “record is there but restricted” scenario: the record exists in the register’s system but is not open to the general public during the confidentiality period.
- Useful when explaining why a register of deeds will confirm existence but deny public access.
