## Practitioner Summary
This section outlines the procedures for disposing of obsolete or unusable county property through competitive bidding, auctions, or public sale.

## Key Practice Points
* **Competitive Bidding**: All purchases and sales that do not require sealed bids must be based on at least three competitive bids.
* **Public Auctions**: Property can be disposed of by public auction or gift to a nonprofit organization approved under state law.

## Statutory Text
All purchases and sales in amounts that do not require bid solicitation pursuant to subsection (c) may be made by the county purchasing agent in the open market without newspaper notice, but shall whenever possible be based upon at least three competitive bids. Requisitions for items estimated to cost an amount such as to require public newspaper notice shall not be subdivided in order to circumvent the requirement for public newspaper notice as herein provided.
