## Practitioner Summary
This foundational tax statute establishes the general ad valorem rule that all real and personal property located in Tennessee is subject to assessment and taxation for state, county, and municipal purposes unless expressly exempted by statute.

## Key Practice Points
* **Universal Assessment**: Establishes the broad rule that all real and personal property is subject to local property tax levies.
* **Exemptions**: Property is only exempt if specifically listed under Part 2 of Chapter 5 (e.g., government property, charitable/religious exemptions).

## Statutory Text
All property, real and personal, shall be assessed for taxation for state, county, and municipal purposes, except such as is declared exempt in part 2 of this chapter, or unless otherwise provided.
