## Practitioner Summary
This section marks the 1978 transition of Tennessee county government. It abolished the old "Quarterly County Courts" and "Magistrates," vesting all legislative authority into the modern **County Legislative Body**. It ensures that any old legal references to "Justices of the Peace" now apply to your current County Commissioners.

## Key Practice Points
* **Consolidation of Power**: All legislative powers formerly held by various county councils or courts are now centralized in the single legislative body.
* **Magistrate Equivalence**: Legally, a "member of the county legislative body" is the successor to the historical "magistrate" or "justice of the peace" in a legislative context.
* **Metropolitan Exception**: This section does not apply to counties with a consolidated Metropolitan government (e.g., Davidson/Nashville).

## Statutory Text
(a) The county legislative body is established as a basic legislative unit... (b) Effective September 1, 1978... the quarterly county court, county council and any other forms of county legislative bodies are abolished... and all legislative powers... are hereby vested in the county legislative body.
