## Practitioner Summary
This brief but foundational statute establishes the ultimate authority of the county legislative body to fund, build, and maintain public infrastructure, including the utilization of inmate labor.

## Key Practice Points
* **General Authority**: The county legislative body holds the power to build, repair, and maintain bridges and highways out of general county funds.
* **Inmate Labor**: Authorizes the use of county jail or workhouse inmates to work upon any public highway under the supervision of a local commissioner or overseer.

## Statutory Text
The county legislative body has the power to build, repair, and maintain bridges and highways, and pay for the building, repair, and maintenance of bridges and highways out of general county funds, and, through commissioners and a superintendent of the county jail or workhouse, has the power to work certain inmates upon any public highway, with the aid and superintendence of the local commissioner and overseer, or independently of them.
