# Tenn. Code Ann. § 10-7-510 — Transfer of criminal case files and evidence for historical purposes

## Plain-language rule

- Under certain conditions, criminal case files and evidence that are **public records** and have **historical significance** may be transferred to an eligible university, museum, or other historical institution.
- The **district attorney** and other specified officials may have roles/conditions in the approval process.
- The statute includes safeguards such as retaining **duplicates** or ensuring continued access and considering time-based factors (including references to **20 years**, depending on the record category/condition).

## Practice notes

- This is not a general “give records away” authority; it is a controlled transfer pathway for historically significant criminal records/evidence.
- Often relevant to archival projects, cold-case historical research, and museum/university collections.
