## Practitioner Summary
This statute prohibits officers, committee members, directors, and other persons with duties related to voting on municipal contracts from having a personal interest in such contracts. It clarifies that direct or indirect interests must be publicly acknowledged.

## Key Practice Points
* **Direct Interest**: Any contract where the officer is directly interested.
* **Indirect Interest**: Contracts where the officer is indirectly interested but not directly so, including sole suppliers of goods/services in municipalities/ counties.

## Statutory Text
It is unlawful for any officer, committee member, director, or other person whose duty it is to vote for, let out, overlook, or in any manner to superintend any work or any contract in which any municipal corporation, county, state, development district, utility district, human resource agency, or other political subdivision created by statute shall or may be interested, to be directly or indirectly interested in such a contract. This prohibition does not apply to voting on budget, appropriation resolution, or tax rate resolution unless the vote is on a specific amendment that pertains to the budget or an appropriation.
