## Practitioner Summary
Part of the County Uniform Highway Law (CUHL), this statute designates the Chief Administrative Officer (Highway Superintendent) as the head of the county highway department with exclusive executive control over road construction, maintenance, equipment inventories, and personnel policies.

## Key Practice Points
* **Exclusive Executive Control**: The chief administrative officer holds sole authority over location, construction, repair, and maintenance of all county roads, bridges, and ferries.
* **Personnel Authority**: The officer determines the total number of employees, establishes job classifications, sets wage scales, and handles all departmental employment decisions.
* **Administrative Records**: Mandates the preservation of financial records, equipment inventories, and maintenance of the official county road list.

## Statutory Text
(a) The chief administrative officer shall be the head of the highway department and shall have general control over the location, relocation, construction, reconstruction, repair and maintenance of the county road systems of the county, including roads designated as county roads under § 13-3-406 and including bridges and ferries, but not including roads and bridges under the supervision of the department of transportation or a municipality.
(b) It is the duty of the chief administrative officer to employ qualified administrative personnel as required to handle all administrative functions, including maintenance of financial records, inventory of equipment, supplies, and materials, preservation of maintenance records, maintenance of the official county road list, and all other functions necessary for the operation of the highway department.
