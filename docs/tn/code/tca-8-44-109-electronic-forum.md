## Practitioner Summary
This section outlines the requirements for governing bodies to allow electronic communication via internet forums, ensuring public access and transparency.

## Key Practice Points
* Governing bodies may use an internet forum for electronic communications if certain conditions are met.
* Public notice of the forum's intended use is required.
* Members must be able to view archived communications at local libraries or meeting buildings.
* Compliance with this section is essential for maintaining open meetings laws.

## Statutory Text
**(a)** A governing body may, but is not required to, allow electronic communication between members by means of a forum over the internet only if the governing body:
1. Ensures that the forum through which the electronic communications are conducted is available to the public at all times other than necessary for technical maintenance or unforeseen technical limitations.
2. Provides adequate public notice of the governing body's intended use of the electronic communication forum.
3. Controls who may communicate through the forum.
4. Controls the archiving of the electronic communications to ensure that they are publicly available for at least one (1) year after the date of the communication, provided access is user-friendly for the public.
5. Provides reasonable access for members of the public to view the forum at local public libraries or meeting buildings.

**(b)** Electronic communications posted to a forum shall not substitute for decision making by the governing body in a meeting held in accordance with this part. Communications between members of a governing body posted to a forum complying with this section shall be deemed to be in compliance with the open meetings laws compiled in this part.

**(c)** Prior to a governing body initially utilizing a forum to allow electronic communications by its members that meets the requirements of this section, including public notice required in subsection (a), the governing body shall file a plan with the office of open records counsel. The plan shall describe how the governing body will ensure compliance with subsection (a). Within thirty (30) days of receipt of the plan, the office of open records counsel shall acknowledge receipt of the plan and report whether or not the plan complies with subsection (a). If compliance is not met, written comments regarding the plan must be provided to the governing body. Until such time as the governing body complies with the comments and receives a report of compliance from the office, it may not establish or utilize such forum.

**(d)** No member participating in an electronic communication pursuant to this section is deemed eligible for per diem for such participation.

**(e)** As used in this section, "governing body" means the elected governing body of a county, city, metropolitan form of government, or school board.
