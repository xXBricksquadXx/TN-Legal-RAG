## Practitioner Summary
This section outlines the modern and effective bookkeeping and accounting system that must be devised by the comptroller of the treasury with approval from the governor, to be used by county officials and agencies receiving and disbursing state or political subdivision revenues.

## Key Practice Points
* **Modern Bookkeeping System**: The comptroller is required to devise a system that meets minimum standards for bookkeeping and accounting.
* **Governor's Approval**: The governor must approve the system devised by the comptroller, ensuring it aligns with statewide requirements.

## Statutory Text
The comptroller of the treasury shall devise a modern and effective bookkeeping and accounting system to be used by all county officials and agencies receiving and disbursing the revenues of the state or any political subdivision thereof, and shall prescribe the minimum standards that shall be required under such system.
