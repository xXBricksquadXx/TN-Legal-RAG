## Practitioner Summary
This section establishes the formal procedure for a county legislative body to fill vacancies in offices. It mandates multiple layers of notice (to members and the public), allows for voter nominations, and strictly prohibits secret balloting. It also addresses the status of commissioners who seek appointment to a vacancy.

## Key Practice Points
* **Public Notice**: Public notice of the vacancy must be published in a newspaper of general circulation at least seven (7) days before the meeting.
* **Voter Participation**: Registered voters must be given an opportunity to submit names for consideration before any vote is taken.
* **Voting Prohibitions**: A member of the legislative body who accepts a nomination for an office is prohibited from voting on that appointment; if appointed, they must resign from the legislative body immediately.
* **Majority Vote Required**: A nominee must receive a majority vote of the entire eligible membership (not just those present) to be appointed.

## Statutory Text
(a)(1) Whenever an office is required to be filled... the county clerk shall provide notice to every member... (2) the presiding officer shall cause public notice to be given in a newspaper... at least seven (7) days prior to the meeting... (b) Before the county legislative body votes... the chair shall allow registered voters... an opportunity to submit names... (e) To receive an appointment, a nominee must receive the votes of a majority of the members... No secret balloting shall be permitted.
