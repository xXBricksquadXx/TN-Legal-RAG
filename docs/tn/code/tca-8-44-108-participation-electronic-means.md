## Practitioner Summary
This statute governs when and how a governing body can hold meetings via electronic means (e.g., video or audio conference). It establishes strict requirements for public access, real-time broadcasting, and recorded minutes to prevent electronic communication from circumventing the Open Meetings Act.

## Key Practice Points
* **Physical Quorum Priority**: Generally, a physical quorum must be present at the location specified in the notice. If not, the body must make a formal "determination of necessity" on the record.
* **Public Access**: If electronic participation is used, the public must be allowed to view/listen in real time, and the body must post a recording of the meeting to its website within 3 business days.
* **Roll Call Votes**: All votes taken during an electronic meeting must be by roll call vote.

## Statutory Text
(a) As used in this section, unless the context otherwise requires:
(1) "Electronic means of communication" means communication by video conference or audio conference and may include the use of an internet-based platform, but does not include email;
(4) "Necessity" means that the matters to be considered by the governing body at that meeting require timely action by the body, that physical presence by a quorum of the members is not practical within the period of time requiring action, and that participation by a quorum of the members by electronic or other means of communication is necessary.
(b)(1) A governing body may, but is not required to, allow participation by electronic or other means of communication for the benefit of the public and the governing body in connection with any meeting authorized by law; provided, that a physical quorum is present at the location specified in the notice of the meeting as the location of the meeting.
(2) If a physical quorum is not present at the location of a meeting of a governing body, then in order for a quorum of members to participate by electronic or other means of communication, the governing body must make a determination that a necessity exists. Such determination, and a recitation of the facts and circumstances on which it was based, must be included in the minutes of the meeting.
(c)(1) Any meeting held pursuant to the terms of this section shall comply with the requirements of the Open Meetings Law, codified in this part, and shall not circumvent the spirit or requirements of that law.
(2) If a meeting will be conducted permitting participation by members by electronic means of communication, then the governing body shall allow members of the public who are not in attendance at the physical location of the meeting to: (A) View and listen to the meeting by electronic means in real time...
(4) A governing body that conducts a meeting allowing participation by electronic means of communication shall make a recording of the meeting, and post the recording or a link to the recording on its website... no later than three (3) business days after the meeting.
(7) All votes taken during a meeting held pursuant to the terms of this section shall be by roll call vote.
