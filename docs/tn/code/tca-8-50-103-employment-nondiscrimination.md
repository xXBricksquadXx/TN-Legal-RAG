## Practitioner Summary
This section, cited as the "Tennessee Disability Act," prohibits state agencies, political subdivisions (counties and cities), and private employers from discriminating against job applicants or employees based solely on physical, mental, or visual disabilities, or the use of a guide dog.

## Key Practice Points
* **Prohibition on Disability Discrimination**: Public and private employers cannot discriminate in hiring, firing, or terms of employment based on physical, mental, or visual disability unless the disability prevents job performance.
* **Public Entities Covered**: Applies fully to counties, municipalities, and all political subdivisions of the state.
* **Enforcement & Remedies**: Violations trigger administrative procedures and civil remedies through the Tennessee Human Rights Commission pursuant to T.C.A. §§ 4-21-302 through 4-21-311.

## Statutory Text
(a) This section and § 8-50-104 shall be known and may be cited as the "Tennessee Disability Act."
(b) There shall be no discrimination in the hiring, firing and other terms and conditions of employment of the state of Tennessee or any department, agency, institution or political subdivision of the state, or of any private employer, against any applicant for employment based solely upon any physical, mental or visual disability of the applicant, unless such disability to some degree prevents the applicant from performing the duties required by the employment sought or impairs the performance of the work involved. Furthermore, no blind person shall be discriminated against in any such employment practices because such person uses a guide dog.
(c) A violation of this subsection (b) is a Class C misdemeanor.
