---
title: TCA 5-9-101
topic: county_finance
jurisdiction: TN
---
The county legislative body may appropriate moneys for miscellaneous purposes including:
(1) Payment of jurors and criminal prosecution costs;
(2) Support of indigent persons or those with mental illness/disabilities;
(11) Building and repairing courthouses and jails;
(21) Sheriff equipment and deputy salaries;
(23) Nonprofit volunteer fire departments.
