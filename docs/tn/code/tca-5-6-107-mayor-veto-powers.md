---
title: TCA 5-6-107
topic: county_executive
jurisdiction: TN
---
(a) The county mayor has the power of veto with respect to legislative resolutions of the county legislative body. 
(b) If the mayor vetoes a resolution, it becomes effective only upon subsequent passage by a majority of all members of the body. Such override must occur within 20 days or at the next regular meeting.
(c) The veto may not be exercised with respect to specific items in the annual county budget; it must be exercised with respect to the whole.
(d) If the mayor fails to sign or veto within 10 days, the resolution becomes effective without signature.
