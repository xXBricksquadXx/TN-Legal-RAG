## Practitioner Summary
This statute establishes the foundational, mandatory duties of a county sheriff in Tennessee. It delineates objective responsibilities regarding the execution of court processes, courthouse security, jail administration, and mandatory fingerprinting and booking procedures.

## Key Practice Points
* **Court and Process Duties**: The sheriff must execute all lawful writs and process, attend all courts held in the county, and maintain order in the courthouse.
* **Jail Custody**: The sheriff has mandatory charge and custody of the county jail and its prisoners, unless a specific interlocal agreement delegates this to a shared facility.
* **Fingerprinting Mandates**: The sheriff must take a full set of fingerprints for incarcerated arrestees and electronically or manually transmit them to the TBI and FBI.
* **Municipal Ordinances**: A sheriff may enforce municipal ordinances only if the municipality expressly requests it by ordinance and files a certified copy with the sheriff and general sessions court.

## Statutory Text
(a) It is the sheriff's duty to:
(1) Execute and return, according to law, the process and orders of the courts of record of this state, and of officers of competent authority, with due diligence, when delivered to the sheriff for that purpose;
(2)(A) Except as provided in subdivision (a)(2)(B), attend upon all the courts held in the county when in session; cause the courthouse or courtroom to be kept in order for the accommodation of the courts; furnish them with fire and water; and obey the lawful orders and directions of the court;
(3) Take charge and custody of the jail of the sheriff's county, and of the prisoners therein; receive those lawfully committed, and keep them personally, or by deputies or jailer, until discharged by law;
(5)(A) Execute all writs and other process legally issued and directed to the sheriff, within the county, and make due return thereof;
(34) Enforce the ordinances of a municipality; provided, that the municipality has expressed by ordinance its intent to have the sheriff enforce its ordinances, and that the municipality has filed a certified copy of its ordinances with the sheriff and the general sessions court of the county;
(35)(A) Take or cause to be taken a full set of fingerprints of each person arrested whether by warrant or capias for an offense which results in such person's incarceration in a jail facility or the person's posting of a bond to avoid incarceration.
