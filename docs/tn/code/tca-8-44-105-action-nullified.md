## Practitioner Summary
This is the statutory "kill switch" for the Tennessee Open Meetings Act. It provides the primary enforcement mechanism by declaring that any governmental action taken during a meeting that violates TOMA is entirely void and legally ineffective.

## Key Practice Points
* **Absolute Nullification**: If a board makes a decision in secret, without a quorum, or without adequate public notice, that decision is legally void.
* **Public Debt Exception**: The only exception to nullification is for otherwise legal commitments that affect public debt (to protect municipal credit/bond markets).

## Statutory Text
Any action taken at a meeting in violation of this part shall be void and of no effect; provided, that this nullification of actions taken at such meetings shall not apply to any commitment, otherwise legal, affecting the public debt of the entity concerned.
