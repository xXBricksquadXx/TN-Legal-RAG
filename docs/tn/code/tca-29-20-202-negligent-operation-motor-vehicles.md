## Practitioner Summary
This is a primary exception to the Tennessee Governmental Tort Liability Act (GTLA). It expressly strips a governmental entity's sovereign immunity when an employee negligently operates a motor vehicle or equipment within the scope of their employment.

## Key Practice Points
* **Express Removal of Immunity**: A county is subject to civil liability if an employee acts negligently while driving a county-owned motor vehicle or operating equipment in the scope of employment.
* **Retention of Emergency Privileges**: The statute expressly preserves the immunities and traffic privileges granted to emergency vehicles under T.C.A. §§ 55-8-101 and 55-8-108.

## Statutory Text
(a) Immunity from suit of all governmental entities is removed for injuries resulting from the negligent operation by any employee of a motor vehicle or other equipment while in the scope of employment.

(b) This section shall not act as a repeal of § 55-8-101, § 55-8-108, or § 55-8-132, and the immunities provided by these sections are hereby expressly continued.
