---
title: TCA 10-7-605
topic: sunshine
jurisdiction: TN
---
The chair of an existing agency shall notify the secretary of a vacancy scheduled to occur in the agency as a result of the expiration of membership terms, at least forty-five (45) days before the vacancy occurs. The chair shall notify the secretary of other vacancies within fifteen (15) days of occurrence. Monthly, the secretary shall publish a list of all vacancies. Notice of a vacancy shall be published until the appointing authority notifies the secretary that the vacancy has been filled.
