# Tenn. Code Ann. § 10-7-601 — Open Appointments Act (short title)

## What this section does (plain-English)

This section provides the short title: §§ 10-7-601 through 10-7-606 may be cited as the **“Open Appointments Act.”**

## Statutory text

Sections 10-7-601 - 10-7-606 shall be known and may be cited as the "Open Appointments Act."

Acts 1992, ch. 766, § 2.
