---
title: TCA 10-7-607
topic: sunshine
jurisdiction: TN
---
(a) It is the intent of the general assembly to recognize the importance of balance in the appointment of minority and non-minority persons to membership on statutorily created decision-making and regulatory boards.
(b) Appointing authorities should make a conscientious effort to select qualified persons so that membership accurately reflects the proportion of each minority group in the population of the state or local area, based on the most recent federal census.
(c) Appointing authorities must submit a report to the secretary of state annually by December 1 disclosing the race, ethnicity, and gender of appointees.
