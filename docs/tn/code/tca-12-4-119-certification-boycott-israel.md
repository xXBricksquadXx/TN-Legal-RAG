## Practitioner Summary
This procurement statute prohibits the state and its political subdivisions (including counties) from entering into major contracts with companies engaged in a boycott of Israel. Failure to include the statutory certification clause renders the contract legally void.

## Key Practice Points
* **Mandatory Certification**: Public entities must ensure any contract to acquire or dispose of services, supplies, IT, or construction includes a written certification that the vendor is not engaged in a boycott of Israel.
* **Threshold Applicability**: This requirement only applies to contracts with a total potential value of $250,000 or more, and only to vendors with ten (10) or more employees.
* **Strict Penalty**: Any contract entered into on or after July 1, 2022, that lacks this certification is void.

## Statutory Text
(b) A public entity shall not enter into a contract with a company to acquire or dispose of services, supplies, information technology, or construction unless the contract includes a written certification that the company is not currently engaged in, and will not for the duration of the contract engage in, a boycott of Israel.
(c) This section does not apply to a contract with a total potential value of less than two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) or to contractors with less than ten (10) employees.
(d) A contract entered into on or after July 1, 2022, that fails to comply with this section is void.
