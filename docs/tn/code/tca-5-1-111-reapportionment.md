## Practitioner Summary
This section mandates that county districts be redrawn at least every 10 years to ensure "substantially equal populations." It provides the criteria for districts (compact and contiguous) and gives citizens the right to challenge an unfair map in Chancery Court.

## Key Practice Points
* **The 10-Year Cycle**: Redistricting must happen at least once every decade following the federal census.
* **Compact & Contiguous**: Districts cannot overlap and must be reasonably compact. No splitting of precincts is allowed except under very specific certifications.
* **Judicial Review**: Any citizen can apply to the Chancery Court to review and, if necessary, overturn an unfair apportionment.

## Statutory Text
(a)(1) ...county legislative bodies... shall meet and... change the boundaries of districts... so that the members represent substantially equal populations. (e) Districts shall be reasonably compact and contiguous and shall not overlap. (g) Upon application of any citizen... the chancery court... shall have original jurisdiction to review the county legislative body's apportionment.
