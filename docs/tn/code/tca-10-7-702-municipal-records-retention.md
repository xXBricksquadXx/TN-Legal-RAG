---
title: TCA 10-7-702
topic: sunshine
jurisdiction: TN
---
(a) The municipal technical advisory service (MTAS) is authorized to compile records retention manuals as guides for municipal officials.
(b) Municipalities may, by resolution, authorize the disposal of permanent paper records provided they have been reproduced via microfilm, computer media, or appropriate electronic medium in accordance with § 10-7-121. Disposal includes destruction of the record.
