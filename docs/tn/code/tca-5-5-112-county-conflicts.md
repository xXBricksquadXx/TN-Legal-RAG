## Practitioner Summary
This section regulates voting by county commissioners who are also county employees (or whose spouses are). It defines specific conflicts of interest related to pay and benefits and establishes a mechanism for voiding improper votes.

## Key Practice Points
* **Direct Conflict**: A member cannot vote on matters that would increase their own pay/benefits or those of their spouse.
* **Voiding Votes**: If the chair finds a member voted in violation of this section, the chair shall declare that member's vote void before the final result is announced.
* **Budget Exceptions**: Members may generally vote on the overall budget or tax rate unless the vote is on a specific amendment in which they have a conflict.
* **Disclosure**: If a conflict exists during a budget vote, the member must declare the conflict at the meeting prior to casting their vote.

## Statutory Text
(a)(1) No member... who is also an employee of the same county or whose spouse is an employee... may vote on matters in which such member has a conflict of interest... (3) The vote of any member having a conflict of interest is void if successfully challenged... (b)(1) Subsection (a) shall not prohibit a member... from voting on the budget... unless the vote is on a specific amendment... (c) Any member... who abstains from voting for cause... shall not be counted for the purpose of determining a majority vote.
