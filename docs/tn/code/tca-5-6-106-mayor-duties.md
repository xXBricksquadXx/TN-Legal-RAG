## Practitioner Summary
This section defines the County Mayor as the **Chief Executive Officer**. It outlines their "Nonvoting Ex Officio" status on committees and their power to appoint department heads, subject to the Commission's "Full and Free" confirmation process.

## Key Practice Points
* **Ex Officio Status**: The Mayor (or a rep) is a nonvoting member of every committee, board, or commission unless otherwise stated by law.
* **Appointment & Confirmation**: The Mayor appoints department heads, but the Commission must confirm them.
* **Separation of Powers**: The Commission is explicitly forbidden from interviewing prospective employees *prior* to the Mayor making the formal appointment.

## Statutory Text
(a) The county mayor shall be the chief executive officer of the county... (b)(1)(A) The county mayor shall serve as a nonvoting ex officio member of the county legislative body... (c) ...the county mayor shall appoint members of county boards and commissions and county department heads. Such appointees shall be subject to confirmation...
