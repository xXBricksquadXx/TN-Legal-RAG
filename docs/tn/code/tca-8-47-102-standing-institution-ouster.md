## Practitioner Summary
This statute authorizes the Attorney General and Reporter, as well as District Attorneys General, county attorneys, and city attorneys, to initiate ouster proceedings against public officers within their respective jurisdictions on their own initiative without needing prior citizen complaints or formal requests.

## Key Practice Points
* **Prosecutorial Authority**: District attorneys, county attorneys, and city attorneys have explicit standing to initiate ouster actions against local officials.
* **Initiative Power**: Prosecutors may act independently on their own initiative without requiring prior citizen petitions or state-level authorization.

## Statutory Text
The attorney general and reporter has the power, on the attorney general and reporter's own initiative, and without any complaint having been made to the attorney general and reporter or request made of the attorney general and reporter, to institute proceedings in ouster against any and all state, county, and municipal officers, under this chapter, and the district attorneys general, county attorneys, and city attorneys, within their respective jurisdictions, may institute such actions, without complaint being made to them or request made of them, as they are authorized to institute upon request made of them or complaint made to them.
