## Practitioner Summary
This statute creates the County Budget Committee and defines its composition. It places the County Mayor at the center of the budgetary process as the ex officio chair, alongside members appointed with the approval of the governing body.

## Key Practice Points
* **Composition**: Generally consists of five members, including the County Mayor.
* **Eligibility**: Members of the budget committee are not required to be members of the county legislative body.
* **Ex Officio Roles**: The County Mayor is the chair; the director of accounts and budgets is the secretary.
* **Duties**: The committee assumes all duties previously held by finance or tax levy committees regarding appropriations.

## Statutory Text
(a) A county budget committee is hereby created. (b)(1) ...the committee shall consist of five (5) members, one (1) of whom shall be the county mayor... (3) The county mayor shall be the ex officio chair... (d) The budget committee shall perform all the duties respecting county budgets and appropriations...
