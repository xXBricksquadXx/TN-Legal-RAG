## Practitioner Summary
This section defines the County Mayor's veto power over legislative resolutions. It distinguishes between legislative and administrative actions and sets strict timelines for both the Mayor’s action and the Commission’s potential override.

## Key Practice Points
* **Legislative Only**: The veto applies only to legislative resolutions, not administrative or appellate actions.
* **Timeline**: The Mayor has ten (10) days to sign or veto a resolution. If they take no action, it becomes effective without their signature.
* **Override Threshold**: To override a veto, the legislative body must pass the resolution again by a majority of the entire membership.
* **Budget Veto Limits**: The Mayor cannot veto specific line items in the annual budget; they must veto the budget as a whole.

## Statutory Text
(a)(1) The county mayor has the power of veto with respect to resolutions... (b)(3) If the county mayor vetoes the resolution... it shall become effective only upon subsequent passage by a majority of all the members... (c) The veto may not be exercised with respect to specific items or parts of items in the annual county budget, and may only be exercised with respect to the whole.
