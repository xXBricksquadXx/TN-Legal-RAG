## Practitioner Summary
Affirmed the robust protections of the Tennessee Right to Farm Act (TCA § 43-26-103). The Court held that an established farm is immune from nuisance suits by residential newcomers if the farm was in operation for at least one year and is not negligent.

## Key Practice Points
* **Priority of Use**: Being "first in time" is the primary defense against encroaching residential development.
* **Negligence Requirement**: Nuisance claims are barred unless the plaintiff can prove the farm is operating in a negligent manner.
* **Statutory Shield**: Reaffirms legislative intent to protect agricultural production from suburban sprawl.

## Case Facts & Disposition
Homeowners sued an adjacent poultry farm for odor and noise.
**Disposition**: Dismissed under the Right to Farm Act.
