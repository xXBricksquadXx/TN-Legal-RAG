## Practitioner Summary
Clarified TCA § 5-1-111 regarding municipal annexation. The Court ruled that a County Legislative Body retains the authority to levy property taxes on annexed territory for the purpose of retiring debt incurred by the county prior to the date of annexation.

## Key Practice Points
* **Pre-existing Debt**: Counties are protected from losing debt-service revenue when cities annex county land.
* **Taxing Authority**: Title 5 powers are not unilaterally extinguished by municipal action regarding bonded indebtedness.
* **County-Municipal Conflict**: Cities cannot "annex away" the county's right to pay off its existing bonds.

## Case Facts & Disposition
Chattanooga annexed land and claimed the county could no longer levy a debt-service tax on those residents.
**Disposition**: Judgment for Hamilton County.
