## Practitioner Summary
The Court held that commercial landscaping/hardscaping is NOT "agricultural labor" under TCA § 50-6-106. Employers in the landscaping industry cannot claim the "farm laborer" exemption to avoid Workers' Compensation liability, even if they handle plants/soil.

## Key Practice Points
* **Exemption Strictness**: The "Agricultural Labor" exemption (TCA § 50-7-207) is applied narrowly.
* **Primary Enterprise Test**: The court looks at the nature of the business (commercial service) rather than the individual task (planting).
* **Liability**: Landscaping firms must carry Workers' Comp regardless of "farm-like" tasks.

## Case Facts & Disposition
Worker injured while installing a stone retaining wall. Employer claimed farm-laborer exemption.
**Disposition**: Employer found liable; landscaping is not farming.
